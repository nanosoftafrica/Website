# Nanosoft website: guide for the marketing team

This guide shows you how to update the Nanosoft Technologies Limited website yourself. You don't need any coding.

## 1. Log in

1. Open the website in your browser and add `/admin/login` to the end of the address.
2. Enter the username and password the web team gave you. Never share them or write them in chat, email or documents.
3. You land on **What would you like to do?**. Pick a task.

A marketing account only sees the **Start** and **Website** menus. That is normal.

## 2. What each Website screen changes

Every screen has a help line at the top that explains what it changes. After you save, most screens show **View on site**. Click it to check your change on the live site.

| Screen | What it changes on the site |
|---|---|
| Home page | Home page headline, About text, client logos, company details, contact details and social links. Click **Save all** when you're done. |
| Products | The product pages (Florena, Nano Pay HR, HireHub, NanoAssetSuite). Untick **Active** to hide one. |
| Product categories | The groups products are shown in on /products. |
| Blog | Articles on /blog. **Published** posts are public. **Draft** posts stay hidden. |
| Case studies | Client stories on /case-studies. |
| Events | The timeline in the About section of the home page. |
| Testimonials | Customer quotes on the home page. |
| Team | People on the /team and /about pages. Untick **Active** to hide someone. |
| Pages | Extra pages such as /about. A page with slug `about` opens at /about. |
| Top menu | The links in the menu bar at the top of the site. |
| Images | Your picture library. You can use any picture in it on the other screens. |

## 3. Common tasks

**Add a client logo**
1. Upload the logo on **Images** (see the image tips below).
2. Open **Home page** and go to the **Clients** section.
3. Click **+ Add client**, type the name and pick the logo, then click **Save all**.
4. Click **View on site** to check it.

**Edit a product**
1. Open **Products** and click **Edit** next to the product.
2. Change the text, features or picture.
3. Make sure **Active** is ticked, then save.
4. Click **View on site**. Each product page already has **Get a Free Demo** buttons.

**Post a blog**
1. Open **Blog** and click **+ New post** (or use **Post a blog** on the start page).
2. Add a title, the text and a cover image.
3. Set **Status** to published when it's ready. Leave it as draft to save it without showing it.
4. Save, then click **View on site**.

**Update social links or contact details**
1. Open **Home page** and go to the **Company** section.
2. Change Phone, Email, Facebook, LinkedIn or Instagram.
3. Click **Save all**. The new details show on the site.

## 4. Image tips

- Use JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP files.
- The limit is 5 MB per file. Make big photos smaller before you upload them.
- SVG files are not accepted.
- Logos look best as PNG with a clear background.
- Give each file a clear name, for example `client-acme-logo.png`.

## 5. Demo requests

Requests from the **Book a demo** form go to **Demo requests and messages**. Only full-access accounts can see that screen. If you need a request followed up, ask the web team.

## 6. What NOT to touch

- **Settings** and **Tags**. These are for the web team only.
- CRM, sales and support screens. They don't change the website.
- Integrations and anything about passwords, users or roles.
- Don't delete products, pages or team members. Untick **Active** or set **Draft** to hide them instead.

If you're not sure, stop and ask first.
